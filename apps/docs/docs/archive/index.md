Archive stores historical engineering records that remain useful for context:

- Research spikes and exploratory architecture notes
- Implementation plans and transition documents
- Handoffs and postmortems
- Superseded but informative proposals

Archive pages are for historical reference and may not reflect current production behavior.

For deep operational records, use [Cold Archive](/archive/cold).
