💡 What: Added accessible `htmlFor` and `id` bindings to the Temperature slider and Tag input in the Prompt Editor. Also added `aria-hidden="true"` to the internal `Plus` SVGs of the "Add tag" icon buttons in both the Prompt Editor and the Sidebar.

🎯 Why: To ensure screen readers can accurately associate labels with their respective form controls, and to prevent redundant or confusing announcements from decorative SVGs inside icon-only buttons.

📸 Before/After: Visuals remain unchanged. Screen readers now properly announce "Temperature slider" and "Tags input".

♿ Accessibility:
- Mapped `<Label htmlFor="temperature-slider">` to `<input id="temperature-slider">`.
- Mapped `<Label htmlFor="tag-input">` to `<Input id="tag-input">`.
- Set `aria-hidden="true"` on `<Plus>` icons within `<Button aria-label="Add tag">`.
